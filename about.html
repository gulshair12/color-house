About

We are the Leader in Construction Industry

Build Your Dreams, Our Commitment

Color House Building Contracting LLC was established in Dubai in 2023, backed by extensive experience in the construction and contracting industry. Before establishing our company in the UAE, our team gained more than 10 years of valuable experience in Oman, where we successfully handled and completed various contracting and construction projects.

In 2023, we brought this experience and expertise to Dubai, one of the region’s most dynamic and rapidly developing markets. Since our establishment, we have successfully completed and handed over projects within a short period, demonstrating our commitment to quality, professionalism, timely delivery, and customer satisfaction.
